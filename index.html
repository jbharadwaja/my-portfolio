01

Baseline first.

Every model starts against a dumb baseline. If it can't beat the median, I say so — gold recovery scored 9.41 sMAPE against a 9.44 baseline.

02

Features over tricks.

Lags, rolling means, TF-IDF, lemmatized text. Most of the lift lives in the features.

03

Validate like I mean it.

Cross-validation, held-out test sets, 1,000-sample bootstraps. When a reviewer found leakage, I refit on training data only.

Plain linear regression beat LightGBM and CatBoost on taxi demand. The README says so.
